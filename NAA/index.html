Our game Mars: Last Colony uses captivating and educational gameplay to teach users how to plan and structure a NASA space mission, how to land a shuttle on Mars, and how to sustain life on Mars.

Throughout the game, players are met with challenges and decisions that impact the future of their colony.

1

Mission Design Lab

Choose a science goal, a spacecraft and a rocket, then catch the launch window to Mars, the way NASA does.

Stage 1 · inside the lab

Every choice in 3D

Pick a science goal, then build your spacecraft piece by piece. Each instrument appears on it, it rides inside the rocket you choose, and every mission you launch is saved and scored.

2

Landing

Pick one of five landing sites, fall from orbit, open the parachute and fly the final descent yourself. Touch down softly.

Stage 2 · where you land

Five landing sites

Each site is inspired by a real place on Mars, and each looks and plays differently.

3

Sustain

Keep oxygen, water, food and power flowing. Find water in an ice biome of blue crystal mountains, survive storms and quakes, and grow a colony.

2026 · Space Mission Design Game

NASA Space Apps Challenge

The challenge

Create an interactive game where players design, manage and simulate a complete space mission: make engineering decisions, manage limited resources such as mass, power and budget, and see how every choice shapes the mission’s success. Alone or as a team of up to four.

Our objective

Team NAA set out to teach anyone how NASA plans a mission, how a spacecraft lands on Mars and how a crew stays alive there, using real NASA data and decisions that really matter, in a game fun enough to keep playing.
