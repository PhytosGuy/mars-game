Mars: Last Colony

Plan the mission. Land on Mars. Sustain the colony.

Play free Discover

Our game Mars: Last Colony uses captivating and educational gameplay to teach users how to plan and structure a NASA space mission, how to land a shuttle on Mars, and how to sustain life on Mars.

Throughout the game, players are met with challenges and decisions that impact the future of their colony.

1

Mission Design Lab

Choose a science goal, a spacecraft and a rocket, then catch the launch window to Mars, the way NASA does.

2

Landing

Fall from orbit, open the parachute and fly the final descent yourself. Touch down softly.

3

Sustain

Keep oxygen, water, food and power flowing. Survive storms and quakes, and grow a colony.
